## What is the average percentage of body weight loss in adults without diabetes treated with semaglutide in randomized controlled trials?

In the SELECT trial, adults without diabetes treated with semaglutide lost an average of 10.2% of their body weight over 208 weeks. [^1]

## What is the duration of treatment and follow‑up period used in the major semaglutide weight‑loss trials for adults without diabetes?

The STEP‑1 trial treated adults without diabetes for 68 weeks. [^2]

The STEP‑3 trial also treated adults without diabetes for 68 weeks. [^2]

The STEP‑5 trial treated adults without diabetes for 104 weeks. [^2]
