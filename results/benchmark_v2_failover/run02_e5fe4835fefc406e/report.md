## What criteria does the EU AI Act use to classify an AI system as high‑risk?

An AI system is classified as high‑risk if it is intended as a safety component of a product or is itself a product covered by Annex I and requires a third‑party conformity assessment, or if it is listed in Annex III. [^1]

## Which specific AI applications or sectors are listed as high‑risk under the EU AI Act?

The EU AI Act lists eight high‑risk sectors in Annex III: biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, essential services \(including credit scoring and insurance\), law enforcement, migration, and justice. [^2]

## When do the compliance obligations for high‑risk AI systems under the EU AI Act become legally binding?

The EU AI Act’s general application date, making all compliance obligations legally binding, is 2 August 2026. [^3]

High‑risk AI systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 are exempt from the new obligations unless they undergo a significant redesign after that date. [^4]
