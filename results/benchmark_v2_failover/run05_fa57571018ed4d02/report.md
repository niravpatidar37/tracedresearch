# Research report

**Question:** How do sodium-ion batteries compare with lithium-ion batteries on cost, energy density and commercial deployment?

## Summary

Commercial sodium‑ion batteries in 2024 exhibit gravimetric energy densities ranging from 100–150 Wh/kg to 458 Wh/kg, while lithium‑ion cells typically achieve 396 Wh/kg or higher, indicating a wide variance in sodium‑ion performance. [^1][^2]

## Energy Density Comparison

Commercial sodium‑ion cells reported by one source achieve a gravimetric energy density of about 458 Wh/kg, approaching the 396 Wh/kg of older sodium‑ion designs and nearing lithium‑ion levels. [^1]

Another source reports typical sodium‑ion gravimetric energy densities in the 100–150 Wh/kg range, with prismatic cells reaching 130–160 Wh/kg, which is substantially lower than most lithium‑ion chemistries. [^2]
