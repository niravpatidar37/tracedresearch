# Research report

**Question:** What is known about microplastics in human tissue and their health effects?

## Summary

Microplastic concentrations have been quantified in several human tissues, with blood, lung, and stool showing measurable levels. [^1]

## Concentrations in Human Tissues

Blood contains about 1.6 micrograms of plastic per milliliter, based on a 2022 study of 22 healthy volunteers. [^1]

Lung tissue shows a median of 2.19 particles per gram, with diseased lungs averaging 4.31 particles per gram versus 0.47 in controls; 12 plastic types were identified among 108 particles. [^1]

Stool samples reveal a median of 3.5 microplastic particles per gram, ranging from 50 to 500 micrometers and comprising up to nine plastic types. [^1]
