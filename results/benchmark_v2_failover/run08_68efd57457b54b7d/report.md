## How did JWST’s spectroscopic measurements refine the estimated ages and star-formation rates of the earliest galaxies?

JWST/NIRSpec spectra show that the galaxies A1703‑zd6 and RXCJ2248‑ID are undergoing very young starbursts with stellar populations younger than about 2 Myr. [^1]

## What evidence from JWST imaging has altered theories about the formation of the first massive galaxies?

JWST imaging shows that massive early galaxies were already much larger and more massive than predicted, implying they formed and evolved faster than models allow. [^2]

The mass estimates of these early galaxies may be underestimated by a factor of about four if they have bottom-heavy initial mass functions. [^2]

JWST data reveal that early galaxies contain many more faint, low‑mass stars than previously thought, making them three to four times more massive than earlier estimates. [^3]

The discovery of such massive, mature galaxies so soon after the Big Bang challenges existing theories of how the first galaxies formed and evolved. [^3]
