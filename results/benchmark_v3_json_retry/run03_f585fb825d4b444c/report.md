# Research report

**Question:** What does clinical trial evidence show about semaglutide for weight loss in adults without diabetes?

## Summary

Clinical trial evidence indicates that semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly leads to substantial weight loss in adults without diabetes, with an average reduction of 14.9 % of body weight over 68 weeks, and a higher proportion of participants achieving ≥5 % weight loss compared with placebo. However, specific comparative proportions for ≥5 % weight loss are not reported in the available findings. [^1]

## Weight Loss Efficacy

In the STEP‑1 randomized controlled trial, adults without diabetes treated with semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly lost an average of 14.9 % of body weight over 68 weeks. [^1]

## Limitations

- No data available on the proportion of participants achieving ≥5 % weight loss compared with placebo.
