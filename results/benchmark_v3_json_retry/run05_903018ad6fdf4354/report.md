## What is the current cost per kWh of sodium‑ion batteries compared to lithium‑ion batteries?

In 2024 sodium‑ion batteries are projected to cost about $85 per kWh, slightly lower than lithium‑ion batteries at $89 per kWh. [^1]

The average cost for sodium‑ion cells in 2024 is $87 per kWh, marginally cheaper than lithium‑ion cells at $89 per kWh. [^2]

CATL’s sodium‑ion cells are priced at $50–60 per kWh at the cell level in Q4 2024, compared to $80–100 per kWh for its own LFP cells. [^3]

## What are the gravimetric energy densities \(Wh/kg\) of sodium‑ion versus lithium‑ion batteries?

KPIT Technologies’ sodium‑ion batteries have energy densities ranging from 100 Wh/kg to 170 Wh/kg. [^4]

Sodium‑ion cells have a gravimetric energy density of roughly 120–160 Wh/kg at the cell level. [^5]

Lithium‑ion batteries typically achieve gravimetric energy densities between 150 and 250 Wh/kg. [^6]
